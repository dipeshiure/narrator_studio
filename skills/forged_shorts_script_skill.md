# FORGED Shorts Script Skill

English-only discipline / mindset YouTube Shorts for the FORGED channel. Reuse this file whenever you write, rewrite, or QA a FORGED script.

## 日本語まとめ

FORGED（英語・顔出しなし・規律/マインドセットのShorts）の台本を書くときの再利用ルール。狙いは **12–18秒、4–6行、25–40語**。フックは **最初のフレームに全文** を出す。5つの型（VERSUS / POV / 対比の格言 / If-Whenのひねり / 命令か代償）から **1つだけ** 選び、タイトルはフックそのもの（対比・断定・命令）。二人称の短い断定文と具体的なイメージ（スヌーズ、机、アラーム、1年）を使い、売り込み言葉と「登録して」は禁止。画面文字は **Anton・大文字**、セーフエリアを守る。自分のチャンネルで伸びているのは短い（18–22秒）断定タイトル。長い説明・疑問形・柔らかい助言・ハッシュタグ詰めは伸びない。

## 1. Purpose / when to use

Use this skill to write or audit a new FORGED Shorts script before rendering in `narrator_studio` (Mac) or the box pipeline.

- Channel: **FORGED** (English only; faceless dark discipline / mindset).
- Output: hook, body lines, looping ending, title, 2-line description, tags / hashtags (hashtags never go in the spoken script).
- One idea per Short. One hook pattern. Text must make sense with the sound off.
- Do not invent performance numbers. Cite only figures already in the sources listed at the bottom.

Do **not** use this for long-form, Japanese narration, clipping other people's videos, or a different channel's voice.

## 2. What the 2026-11-16/17 research actually showed

Research window: Shorts uploaded roughly the last 60 days as of **2026-11-16 JST**. Method in `hooks_titles_report.md`: 590 Shorts pulled, then filtered to **72 English discipline/mindset Shorts from 47 channels** (64 from channels with ≤100K subs). Numbers are YouTube metadata from that date, not estimates.

**Key findings**

1. In about 90% of the 72 winners, a complete sentence (or a two-label comparison) is readable on frame 0. Nobody builds up to the point.
2. Small-channel breakout is strongest with **VERSUS**: the 8 VERSUS Shorts had a median of **230× views-per-subscriber** (vs 1.3× for plain quotes). Example: One Idea Wider (1,840 subs) — "Obsession bleeds. Discipline walks." — **5,641,696** views.
3. **POV** had the biggest raw-view median in the sample (**830K**), but the top POV hits are often copy-paste clones of one viral clip. Use the *format*, not the clip.
4. Length: sample median **17 s**; middle 50% = **11–22 s**. Relentora (68,400 subs): median **14.5 s**, 37 Shorts in 60 days, median **16,534** views. HurricaneMentalities median **17 s**. Target **12–18 s**, sweet spot about **15 s**.
5. Most winners have **no voiceover** (text + music). FORGED keeps a calm deep male voice, but the on-screen text must carry the Short with sound off.

**FORGED's own channel (8 subscribers, public data as of 2026-11-17 JST)**

Winners are short (**18–22 s**) and the **title is the hook** (contrast, verdict, or command), with concrete images:

| Title | Views | Length | Uploaded |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Disappear for 90 Days | 1,276 | 50 s | 10/18 |

## 3. The five hook patterns

Pick **exactly one** per Short. Line 1 is the hook: **3–9 words**, a complete sentence or a two-label comparison, shown in full on frame 0, spoken in the first 1–2 seconds. It must state the payoff or the conflict immediately (contrast, verdict, or a direct "you"). No question that can be answered "no". No setup.

### 1) VERSUS / labelled comparison

**What frame 0 shows:** two labels side by side, or a short "[A] vs [B]" sentence.

**Hook template:** `[A] vs [B].` or `[A] [verb]. [B] [verb].`

**Title formula:** `[A] vs [B]. Which One Are You?`

**Why it works:** strongest views-per-subscriber pattern for small channels (median 230× in the 72-Short sample).

**Real examples (do not copy wording blindly):**

- "Obsession bleeds. Discipline walks." — 5,641,696 views / 1,840 subs
- "Overthinker vs Doer" — 2,920,550 views / 15,700 subs
- "Comfort vs Discipline" — 371,968 views / 734 subs

**FORGED batch examples:** *Motivation vs Discipline. Which One Are You?* (batch 2); *Overthinker vs Doer. Which One Are You?* (today, 13.2 s).

### 2) POV + one-line scenario

**What frame 0 shows:** `POV: You [situation].`

**Hook template:** `POV: You [concrete situation happening now].`

**Title formula:** `POV: You [Situation]`

**Why it works:** biggest raw-view format in the sample (median 830K). The viral hits are often clones — invent a new situation, preferably one already proven on FORGED (tired / energy / unfinished work), not "act before it's too late."

**Real examples (format only):**

- "POV: Successful people act before it's too late." — 104,444,171 views / 36,600 subs (do not clone)
- Druxo — "POV: Everyone expects you to give up, but your ego has other plans." — 13,509 views / 8,770 subs
- Relentora phone-vs-dream framing — 15,063 views

**FORGED batch examples:** *POV: You Finally Put the Phone Down* (batch 2); *POV: You Wait for Energy That Never Comes* (today, 14.2 s).

### 3) Contrast truth / aphorism

**What frame 0 shows:** a full sentence with a reversal or metaphor inside.

**Hook template:** `[Noun] is [verdict].` or `[Concrete thing] is [metaphor].`

**Title formula:** `[Noun] Is [Verdict]`

**Why it works:** a single sharp truth can break out even on a tiny channel.

**Real examples:**

- "Every snooze is a train you miss" — 829,148 views / 246 subs
- "A focused idiot achieves more than a distracted genius." — Relentora, 22,839 views
- "Lust Is the Final Boss" — 14,828 views / 2,180 subs
- "Comfort Is Slowly Stealing Your Future…" — 18,246 views / 511 subs

**FORGED batch examples:** *Comfort Is a Slow Death.* (batch 2); *Every Snooze Is a Train You Miss* (today, 11.5 s — slightly under the 12 s floor, but inside the sample's 11 s lower quartile).

### 4) If / When twist

**What frame 0 shows:** a conditional that flips instead of comforting.

**Hook template:** `If [situation], [twist].` or `When [situation], [twist].`

**Title formula:** `If [Situation], [Twist].`

**Why it works:** the twist is the hook. "If you're tired, rest" loses; "If you're tired, do it tired" wins. Relentora and RiseForge use this often. Yan-ha Motivation — "If You Are Tired, Then Do It Tired" — 5,916 views / 1,160 subs (in the 72-Short table).

**Real examples:**

- EikO — "If they team up to go against you…" — 226,527 views / 142,000 subs
- Relentora — "If death sat next to you … said 'times up'" — 44,649 views

**FORGED batch examples:** *If Nobody Believes in You, Good.* (batch 2); *If You're Tired, Do It Tired.* (today, 14.1 s) — extends FORGED's own *Tired Is Not a Verdict on the Whole Day* (22 s, 271 views on 11/16).

### 5) Command or consequence

**What frame 0 shows:** a short imperative, often `[Verb] now or [consequence].`

**Hook template:** `[Verb] now or [consequence].` or a 2–6 word command that is also the title.

**Title formula:** `[Verb] Now or [Consequence]`

**Why it works:** HurricaneMentalities built a catalog on 2–5 word command titles that are also the frame-0 caption (older uploads, still worth copying): "Pay Sweat Or Pay Misery" 208,507; "Suffer Now Or Regret" 166,016; "Take The Harder Road" 293,334. FORGED's own #2 video is this theme: unfinished tasks bury the life you want.

**FORGED batch examples:** *Pay Now or Pay Forever.* (batch 2); *Finish It Now or Stay Buried.* (today, 15.1 s).

**Off-brand (do not use):** pattern-interrupt meta jokes ("Keep scrolling man / No naked girl here!"). That format can get views (Goggins Arc, 832,359) but it is not FORGED.

## 4. Story arc and line-by-line templates

Every script follows the same spine, in 4–6 lines:

1. **Hook** (line 1, 3–9 words) — conflict or verdict on frame 0.
2. **Concrete scene / images** (lines 2–3) — things you can see or feel: couch, desk, phone, alarm, cold, one page, nine minutes.
3. **Turn / truth** (line 3 or 4) — cost, time jump, or reversal ("One year later…", "Ten years later…", "Every delay stacks another year.").
4. **Micro-action** (line 4 or 5) — one small thing to do now ("Close one loop tonight.", "One ugly set. One cold page.").
5. **Looping ending** (last line, 2–6 words) — verdict, command, or question that sends the replay back to line 1. Never a subscribe ask.

Escalate: problem → cost → reversal. Each line adds a **new** concrete image or a reversal. Present tense. Second person ("you") or parallel labels. Two-beat parallels are the house rhythm: "Motivation is a feeling. It leaves." / "The alarm. The cold. The extra rep."

### VERSUS — 5–6 lines

```
1 HOOK          [A] vs [B].
2 CONCRETE A    The [A] [visible stalling action].
3 CONCRETE B    The [B] [visible finishing action].
4 TURN          [Time jump]. Only one [moved / finished / stayed].
5 ENDING        Which one are you?
```

Optional extra body line if you need 6 lines: a two-beat pair, "Motivation is a feeling. It leaves." / "Discipline is a decision. It stays." Then the ending.

### POV — 4–5 lines

```
1 HOOK          POV: You [situation that is happening now].
2 SCENE         [Sensory image]. [Second image in the same room.]
3 TURN          You [delay / promise]. [Tomorrow / it] looks the same.
4 ACTION+END    [Short command that answers the POV.]
```

Line 2 can be two fragments in one caption ("The bed feels safe. The clock moves.") if each side stays concrete.

### Contrast truth — 5 lines

```
1 HOOK          [Noun] is [verdict / metaphor].
2 SCENE         [How it shows up today — snooze, skipped workout, delay.]
3 TURN          [Small number + time cost: nine minutes, one year, ten years.]
4 ACTION        [Command in 2–6 words.]
5 ENDING        [Callback to the hook, or a last command.]
```

Lines 4–5 can collapse into one ending if the command already loops ("Get up on the first ring.").

### If / When twist — 5 lines

```
1 HOOK          If [state / situation], [unexpected command or twist].
2 SCENE         The [body / room / desire] [wants the easy image].
3 SCENE         The [work / cost] still [sits / waits] there.
4 ACTION        One [ugly concrete thing]. One [second concrete thing].
5 ENDING        [Do it anyway / callback to the twist].
```

### Command or consequence — 5–6 lines

```
1 HOOK          [Verb] now or [consequence].
2 SCENE         The [unfinished thing] [sits / waits] [where].
3 TURN          Every delay [stacks a year / costs everything at once].
4 ACTION        [One tonight action], or a fragment list: "The alarm. The cold. The extra rep."
5 ENDING        [Verb] it now. / Pay it now.  (must echo line 1)
```

## 5. Pacing and tempo

| Rule | Target |
|---|---|
| Duration | **12–18 s** (aim ~15 s). Sample median was 17 s; Relentora 14.5 s. FORGED's own winners cluster at 18–22 s, so do not run longer than 18 s on new videos unless a test says otherwise. |
| Lines | **4–6** total. Each line = one on-screen caption. |
| Spoken words | **25–40** |
| Words per line | **3–8** (hook may be **3–9**) |
| Ending | **2–6** words |
| Delivery rate | about **2.2–2.5 words/sec** (e.g. ~33–37 words in 15 s, ~26–30 words in 12 s, ~40 words in 18 s) |
| Hook timing | Full hook on **frame 0**; spoken in the first **1–2 s** |
| Dead air | **None** at the start or between lines. narrator_studio has added a default opening pause before — strip it. |

If a cut lands under ~2.0 words/sec, first remove opening silence and tighten pauses. Only then add one more concrete image, still inside 40 words and 18 s. Do not pad with filler adjectives.

One topic only, from: motivation vs discipline, comfort / snooze, phone / scrolling / focus, doubters / being alone, time / regret, pain as the price, ego refusing to quit, lust / dopamine, lock in. Prefer a theme FORGED has already proven (tired, unfinished tasks, time) when pairing it with a fresh hook pattern.

**Avoid repeating recent hooks.** Already used in recent batches: Motivation vs Discipline; POV phone down; Comfort Is a Slow Death; If Nobody Believes in You, Good; Pay Now or Pay Forever; Disappear / 90 days / silence / recognize you; If You're Tired, Do It Tired; Every Snooze Is a Train You Miss; Finish It Now or Stay Buried; Overthinker vs Doer; POV: You Wait for Energy That Never Comes.

## 6. Narration and delivery style

- Deep, calm male voice. No hype, no shout-intro, no "listen up."
- Second person **"you"** / **"your"**, or parallel labels in a VERSUS script.
- Short declaratives and fragments. House examples: "The alarm. The cold. The extra rep." / "One ugly set. One cold page." / "The bed feels safe. The clock moves."
- Present tense. Two-beat parallel structure.
- Small concrete numbers only, never statistics: "nine minutes," "one year," "ten years," "one page," "first ring."
- The voice supports the text; the text must work muted.
- On the Mac tool, narration is CosyVoice 2 ("atlas"). On the box pipeline it has been edge-tts `en-US-ChristopherNeural` at rate -6% and pitch -8Hz. Either way, write for a low, steady read — not a trailer voice.

---

## 7. On-screen text rules

- Font: **Anton**, all caps (or Anton-style heavy caps). Do not go back to a thin or decorative font.
- The **full hook line is on screen from frame 0**.
- One caption per script line. No hashtags, emojis, or brackets in spoken or caption text.
- Safe area on a 1080×1920 frame (from `script_rules.md` / the box renderer):
  - Keep the **top 330 px** clear
  - Keep the **right 180 px** clear
  - Keep the **bottom 20%** clear
  - Practical text box used in the box renderer: x 90–900, y 330–1400
- Captions must stay readable on bright or color backgrounds (dark outline / strong contrast). Soft white-on-light fails.
- The title is the hook (or its sharpest half), so the first frame, the title, and the spoken first line should match.

## 9. Banned phrases and weak patterns

**Openers:** Have you ever, Did you know, In this video, Let me tell you, Imagine a world, Here's why, Listen, Hey, Here's.

**Filler / hype:** journey, unlock your potential, embrace, mindset shift, level up, game-changer, truly, simply, really, incredible, amazing, powerful, ultimate, secret.

**Vague lines with no image:** believe in yourself, stay positive, success is a journey, you got this.

**Also banned:**

- Questions that can be answered "no"
- Sentences over 12 words; lists of 3+ adjectives; semicolons; questions in the middle of the body; explaining the lesson after it has landed
- Hashtags, emojis, or brackets in the spoken script
- "Subscribe", "follow for more", "like if you agree", "thanks for watching"
- Clickbait title words ("SHOCKING", "you won't believe")
- Cloning a viral POV line or using someone else's footage

## 10. Title rules

- Under 60 characters, ideally **3–9 words**.
- The title **is** the hook line or its sharpest half.
- Title Case. Ends with a period, a question mark only when the formula includes "Which One Are You?", or no punctuation.
- Use **one** of these five formulas:

  1. `[A] vs [B]. Which One Are You?`
  2. `POV: You [Situation]`
  3. `[Noun] Is [Verdict]`
  4. `If [Situation], [Twist].`
  5. `[Verb] Now or [Consequence]`

- No clickbait words. Hashtags belong only in the description, not the title or the script.

**Description package:** 2 short lines that repeat the hook and the micro-action, then hashtags including `#shorts`. Keep the set small (e.g. `#shorts #discipline #motivation #lockin`). Do not stuff the title or the first line with tags — that pattern already lost on FORGED.

## 11. How to write a new script

1. **Pick one proven topic** (prefer tired, unfinished tasks, time, comfort/snooze, phone/focus, doubters, pain as price) that is **not** a hook already used in a recent batch (see the avoid-repeat list in §5).
2. **Pick one hook pattern** from the five above. Match topic to a pattern that is still fresh for that topic (today's batch paired FORGED-proven themes with one pattern each).
3. **Write the hook / title first** (3–9 words, formula-compliant). If it does not work with the sound off, rewrite it before writing the body.
4. **Fill the spine:** hook → 1–2 concrete images → turn/truth with a small time or cost number → one micro-action → 2–6 word ending that loops to line 1.
5. **Count.** 4–6 lines, 25–40 words, 3–8 words per line (hook ≤9), ending 2–6. Duration should land 12–18 s at about 2.2–2.5 words/sec.
6. **Cut** any banned opener, filler word, extra idea, or line that only explains the previous line.
7. **Write the title, 2-line description, and 5 hashtags** (include `#shorts`). Title must match the on-screen hook.
8. **Run the QA checklist** in §12. Only then render (Anton, hook on frame 0, no opening dead air, safe area).

**Output shape (paste-ready):**

```
TITLE: ...
PATTERN: Versus | POV | Contrast truth | If/When twist | Command-or-consequence
DURATION TARGET: 12–18s

HOOK:
...

BODY:
...
...

ENDING:
...

DESCRIPTION:
...
...

#shorts #discipline #motivation #lockin
```

## 12. QA checklist (before rendering)

- [ ] Hook is readable and complete on frame 0 (3–9 words)
- [ ] Total 12–18 s, 4–6 lines, 25–40 words, about 2.2–2.5 words/sec
- [ ] Every line ≤8 words (hook ≤9); ending 2–6 words
- [ ] No banned opener, filler, hype word, hashtag, emoji, or subscribe CTA
- [ ] At least one contrast/reversal and one concrete image
- [ ] One idea only; present tense; "you" or parallel labels
- [ ] Ending is a verdict, command, or question that loops to the hook
- [ ] Title uses one of the 5 formulas and matches the on-screen hook
- [ ] Description is 2 lines plus a short hashtag set; no tag stuffing
- [ ] Text stays in the safe area (top 330 px, right 180 px, bottom 20% clear)
- [ ] Font is Anton caps; hook style matches current FORGED Shorts
- [ ] No opening dead air; first image/text is immediate
- [ ] English only; not a repeat of a recent hook

## 13. Three worked examples (today's 2026-11-17 batch)

These are the actual scripts written for `Downloads/FORGED_upload_ready/` (v4). Lengths are from `publish_pack.md`.

### Example A — If / When twist (14.1 s, 5 lines, 26 words)

**Title:** If You're Tired, Do It Tired.  
**Why this one:** Extends FORGED's 11/16 video *Tired Is Not a Verdict on the Whole Day* (22 s, 271 views). Same theme, shorter command, concrete images (couch, ugly set, cold page).

```
HOOK     If you're tired, do it tired.
SCENE    The body wants the couch.
SCENE    The work still sits there.
ACTION   One ugly set. One cold page.
ENDING   Do it tired anyway.
```

The ending loops: "do it tired anyway" sends the replay back to "If you're tired, do it tired."

**Description:** If you're tired, do it tired. The work still sits there. Do the two-minute version.

### Example B — VERSUS (13.2 s, 5 lines, 25 words)

**Title:** Overthinker vs Doer. Which One Are You?  
**Why this one:** VERSUS is the strongest small-channel pattern (STICK TO IT, 2,920,550 views / 15,700 subs). FORGED already tried the theme in *The More You Overthink, the Less You Do* (48 s, 53 views) and it was too long. This version is a 13-second label comparison.

```
HOOK     Overthinker vs doer.
SCENE    The overthinker rewrites the plan again.
SCENE    The doer finishes the first page.
TURN     One year later, only one moved.
ENDING   Which one are you?
```

The last line is the loop and the title's second half.

**Description:** Overthinker vs doer. One rewrites the plan. One finishes the first page.

### Example C — Command or consequence (15.1 s, 5 lines, 25 words)

**Title:** Finish It Now or Stay Buried.  
**Why this one:** FORGED's #2 video, *The Life You Want Is Buried Under Unfinished Tasks* (18 s, 1,127 views). Same buried/unfinished idea, recast as a short command-or-consequence hook.

```
HOOK     Finish it now or stay buried.
SCENE    The half-done task sits on your desk.
TURN     Every delay stacks another year.
ACTION   Close one loop tonight.
ENDING   Finish it now.
```

The ending is a shortened hook, so the loop is exact.

**Description:** Finish it now or stay buried. Close one loop tonight, not later.
