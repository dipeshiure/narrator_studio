Before Mario: The Card Company That Became Nintendo

## A 137-Year-Old Startup
Nintendo is older than the airplane, older than Hollywood, and older than the radio. [[entity:Nintendo headquarters Kyoto]]

It was founded in Kyoto in 1889. And for its first seventy years, it had nothing to do with video games. [[photo:Kyoto old street]]

## Playing Cards
Its founder, Fusajiro Yamauchi, made hanafuda. Small, colorful playing cards with flowers on them. [[photo:Hanafuda cards]]

They were hand-painted, and they sold in shops across Japan. [[query:japanese traditional craft hands]]

## The Big Question
So how does a card maker from Kyoto end up owning the most famous plumber in the world? That is the story nobody outside Japan tells properly. [[query:tokyo city night]]
